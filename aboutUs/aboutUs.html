About the Library

Digitization Initiative

The CMU Library has embarked on a comprehensive digitization project to convert theses, dissertations, research papers, and other resources into digital format. This allows students and faculty to access materials anytime, anywhere, making research faster, easier, and more convenient.

Easy Access for Students

Students can explore the library’s digital collection through the online portal, search by author, course, or keyword, and download materials needed for study and research. The library ensures accessibility while maintaining the integrity of its resources.

Thesis Submission

Submit Your Thesis

Students are encouraged to submit their completed theses through the library portal. Once a thesis is submitted, it officially becomes part of the CMU University Library's collection and is considered the property of the library. This ensures that knowledge is preserved, organized, and accessible to all future students and researchers.

The submission process is simple: fill out the required form, upload your PDF document, and confirm the details. After verification, your thesis is added to the digital archive.
